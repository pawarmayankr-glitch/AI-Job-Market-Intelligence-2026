# AI Job Market Intelligence 2026

## 📊 Project Overview

**AI Job Market Intelligence 2026** is a Data Analytics project that analyzes job-market data to understand trends in AI-related and traditional job roles.

The project focuses on job demand, skills, salaries, companies, countries, employment types, locations, and other job-market patterns.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze AI-related vs traditional job postings
- Understand job category distribution
- Identify companies with higher numbers of job postings
- Analyze job postings by country and location
- Study employment and contract types
- Analyze salary patterns
- Compare salary patterns for AI-related and traditional roles
- Identify frequently demanded job keywords and skills
- Understand skill demand in the job market

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses a job-market dataset containing job posting information such as:

- Job title
- Company
- Location
- Country
- Job category
- Contract type
- Contract time
- Salary range
- Job description
- Job posting date
- AI-related classification
- Currency

**Dataset size:** 1,696 job postings.

## 🛠️ Technologies & Libraries

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project includes:

1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
2. Data cleaning and missing-value analysis
3. AI-related vs traditional job analysis
4. Job category distribution
5. Job postings by company
6. Job postings by country
7. Employment type analysis
8. Salary analysis
9. AI-related vs traditional salary analysis
10. Most demanded job keywords
11. Job location analysis
12. Skills analysis
13. Skill demand analysis

## 📈 Key Insights

The analysis explores relationships between job roles, AI adoption, skills, salaries, employment types, companies, and geographic demand.

> Salary comparisons should be interpreted within their respective currencies and markets rather than treating different currencies as directly comparable.
